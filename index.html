30 YEARS OF EXCELLENCE ESTD. 1998 // IIT BOMBAY

Asia's Largest Science & Technology Festival

Born in 1998 with the vision to provide a platform for student engineers to demonstrate their technical acumen, Techfest has surged into a global movement. Spanning 3 electric days at IIT Bombay, it unites the highest tiers of innovation, from mechanical robotics and aerospace engineering to artificial superintelligence and digital arts.
